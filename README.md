A client-side texture, skin, paint, and WebGL2 visual overhaul manager for ProTanki. Supporting both the **HTML5** and **Flash** clients, TextureSwapper lets you swap hull skins, turret models, shot effects, animated and custom paints, supply boxes, parachutes, and map props—plus unlock real-time shaders, dynamic weather, and 3D skin animations in HTML5—without touching game executables.

- **Shot Effects & Dust Editors**: Custom colors, hues, contrast, lasers, flames, and dust particles for all turrets.
- **Props & Parachutes**: Edit map props, buildings, terrain textures, skyboxes, and supply drop parachutes.
- **Supplies & Gold Boxes**: Holiday gold boxes (2011–2023), new supply boxes, and LC flag pedestals with target selection dialogs.

## How to Use

1. Download and run the latest `TextureSwapper_Setup.exe` from [Releases](https://github.com/Gabriel250903/ProTankiTextureSwapper/releases).
2. Choose **HTML5** or **Flash** in Settings and let the app auto-detect your ProTanki cache.
3. Equip the items you want to swap in ProTanki at least once so the game caches them.
4. **Close ProTanki completely** before swapping.
5. Open TextureSwapper, pick your skins, paints, and ProTanki Pack options, and click **Apply Selected**.
6. Launch the game and play. *(In HTML5, press `F8` for the ProTanki Pack overlay or `F9` for 3D Skin Animations.)*

*To revert back to original game textures, click **Restore Originals** inside the **Backup History** tab.*
